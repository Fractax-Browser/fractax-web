Thanks for upgrading to Premium!

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In a few minutes you'll receive an email from Polar with your license key (it starts with FRX-). Check your spam folder too.

  1. Open Fractax. If you don't have it yet, download it here.
  2. Go to Settings → Your plan (or click the crown in the left bar).
  3. Paste the key in "Already have a license key?" and click Activate.

The same key works on 3 computers. To change payment, download invoices or cancel, use Manage subscription.
